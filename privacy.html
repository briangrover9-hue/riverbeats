RiverBeats privacy

RiverBeats is a fan-made game that plays the public River Beats YouTube library. It is not run by River Beats or YouTube.

What the game stores

  • On your device: your best score, name, progress, starred beats and settings. You can clear these in your browser settings.
  • Leaderboard (public): when you beat your best score, the game posts your name (up to 12 characters), score, world, and the beat that was playing. Anyone can see the name, score and world. Use a nickname.
  • Stars: starring a beat sends the beat ID and title, with no name or email.
  • Email (optional): only if you type it in and tick the box. It is used to send you that beat or news from River Beats, and only the project owner can read it. You can ask to be removed at any time.
  • Abuse limits: the server keeps your network address with each post to stop spam. It is not public.

Third parties

Music plays through the YouTube embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com), which is governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy. Scores and emails are stored in a Supabase database.

Removal requests

To remove a score or email, message River Beats through the River Beats YouTube channel and name the entry.
